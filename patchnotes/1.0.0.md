## Bunnyland 1.0.0 "First Bloom"

The first release.

- **Fifteen fairytale creatures**: Cotton, Strawberry, Moon, Lavender and Snowball Bunnies, the Blossom Fox, Cloud Kitty, Strawberry Mouse, Honey Bear, Moon Deer, Pastel Axolotl, Tiny Swan, Rainbow Butterfly, Cherry Blossom Bird and Dreamy Hamster, each with its own GeckoLib model, animations, sounds and behaviour.
- **Natural spawning** in meadows, flower forests, cherry groves, snowy biomes, forests, rivers and swamps. Day creatures sleep at night, night creatures nap by day.
- **Befriending** with eight favourite treats; sit, follow, petting, naming, leashing and the Pet Carrier.
- **73 variants**, 25 of them rare and born only by breeding (8%, doubled when both parents are overjoyed).
- **Dress-up**: dyeable Pet Collars and Pet Bows, plus the Flower Crown, Tiny Top Hat, Party Hat, Star Clip, Heart Glasses and Cozy Scarf.
- **Pet care**: happiness and moods, Feeding Bowls, Pet Beds in four colours, and three toys.
- **The Pet Journal**: discovery, befriending and rare-variant tracking, with a page for every creature.
- Six advancements and a commented config (`bunnyland-common.toml`, `bunnyland-client.toml`).

- **Multiloader**: one release for Forge and Fabric on 1.20.1, and NeoForge and Fabric on 1.21.1, 26.2 and 26.3.

| Minecraft | Loaders | Also needs |
|---|---|---|

Install on both the server and every client.
