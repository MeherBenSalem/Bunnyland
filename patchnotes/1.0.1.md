## Bunnyland 1.0.1

A bug-fix release.

- **Fixed a crash on Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3** (Fabric and NeoForge): the game crashed as soon as a Bunnyland creature was nearby, most often a Rainbow Butterfly. Creatures were missing the tempt range attribute that these versions need. GeckoLib was not the cause.
- 1.20.1 and 1.21.1 were not affected; their files only change version number.

| Minecraft | Loaders | Also needs |
|---|---|---|
| 1.20.1 | Forge 47.1+, Fabric (Loader 0.16+, Fabric API) | GeckoLib 4 (4.4.9+) |
| 1.21.1 | NeoForge 21.1+, Fabric (Fabric API) | GeckoLib 4 (4.8.4+) |
| 26.2 | NeoForge 26.2, Fabric (Fabric API) | GeckoLib 5 (5.5.5+) |
| 26.3 | NeoForge 26.3, Fabric (Fabric API) | GeckoLib 5 (5.5.7+) |

Install on both the server and every client.
